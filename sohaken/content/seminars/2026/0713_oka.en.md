- Title:
Hadron Spectroscopy: From Gell-Mann's Quark Model to Exotic Hadrons

<!--more-->
Abstract:

Starting from the question of why the quark model has been so successful, this lecture will review the current understanding of the quark structure of hadrons and discuss new insights brought about by recent discoveries of exotic hadrons.
