- Title:
Nuclear Gravitational Form Factor: Mass Radius and D-term

<!--more-->
Abstract:

Using the energy-momentum tensor of even-even nuclei within the relativistic mean-field framework, this seminar will present calculations of the pressure and shear-force distributions inside nuclei, as well as the mass-number dependence and shell effects of the corresponding radii and D-terms.
(Reference: arXiv:2605.12023)
