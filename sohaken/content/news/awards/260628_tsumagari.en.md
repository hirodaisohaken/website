On Sunday, June 28, members of the Department of Physics gathered for a social event centered around a softball tournament. Although we were eliminated in the preliminary round, individual efforts led to us successfully scoring two runs.
We would like to thank everyone who participated, those who stepped in to help as substitute players, and the organizers who planned and managed the event.
